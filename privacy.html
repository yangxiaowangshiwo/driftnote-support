浮念隐私政策 / DriftNote Privacy Policy

简体中文

浮念由 yang wang 发布。本政策自 2026 年 11 月 24 日起生效。

记录和本机设置

浮念 1.0 是一款 iPhone 私人日记，无需注册账号。你主动保存的文字、录音、记录日期、类别、情绪和感受用于显示、播放和回顾你的记录。浮念默认将记录和应用偏好保存在本机应用存储中。应用不会将日记内容发送给开发者，也不使用广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方分析工具。

麦克风和设备认证

只有当你主动开始录音时，浮念才使用麦克风。录音用于生成音频记录，不会被转写。你可以在 iPhone 的「设置」中撤销麦克风权限。撤销权限后仍可查看已有录音、写文字及记录心情。

隐私锁需要你主动开启。身份验证由 iOS 完成，浮念只接收认证结果，不读取你的面容数据、指纹数据或设备密码。解锁后可以在浮念「设置」关闭隐私锁。隐私锁控制应用内访问，不会为手动导出的备份添加独立加密。

Apple 服务和手动备份

记录默认保存在本机。「检查 iCloud 连接」可查看当前 Apple 账户的 iCloud 云盘是否可用。选择「开启 iCloud 同步」后，如果已有记录，你可以核对并选择要加入同步的本机记录，也可以接收当前 Apple 账户中已有的 iCloud 记录。开启后，新保存的记录可以同步。浮念会在同步时核对账户。记录的正文、心情、日期和录音可能保存在你的 iCloud 云盘，接收的记录保存在这台 iPhone 上。你可以在浮念设置中关闭同步。

Apple 的设备备份独立于浮念的记录同步。根据你的 Apple 账户和设备设置，设备备份可能包含浮念的本机数据。Apple 按其隐私政策处理这些服务中的数据。开发者不会收到你的系统备份。

你可以手动导出包含正文、情绪和录音的备份。导出的文件保存在你通过系统选择的位置，可以包括你自行选择的云盘。选定位置的服务商会按其规则处理文件。浮念在你选中备份后读取并验证文件，再将记录合并到本机。导入本身不会上传这些记录。处理备份时会创建临时副本，应用会尝试清理这些副本，清理失败时会提示你重试。

保留、删除和隐私咨询

已保存的记录会留在本机，直到你删除记录或删除应用。打开记录详情，点垃圾桶并确认，即可删除该条本机记录及对应的录音。若附件清理未完成，应用会提示重试。浮念保留本机的删除标记，以便导入旧备份时将恢复的记录标记为恢复副本。iPhone 的「卸载 App」可保留应用数据，如需移除本机应用数据，请选择「删除 App」。删除记录或应用不会移除你单独保存的备份，也不会移除既有设备备份中的副本。请在备份保存位置或相应的系统备份设置中管理这些副本。如果记录已发布到 iCloud，请核对云端副本。本机删除或删除应用不会保证所有云端副本立即移除。

如果你在关闭同步期间从本机删除了先前加入同步的记录，浮念会保留该记录的类别、创建时间和本机删除时间，供你用原 Apple 账户重新开启同步时核对。你可以暂缓决定。只有你逐条确认的删除才会加入该账户的同步队列。

客服邮件与公开网页

如需帮助或有隐私问题，可发邮件至 rac_wang@hotmail.com。客服邮箱会保存你的发件地址、邮件内容及你主动附上的文件，以便回复和跟进。你可以从原发件地址写信要求删除相关往来邮件。请避免附上日记正文、录音或备份文件。

公开支持页和隐私政策由 GitHub Pages 托管。当你在浏览器中访问时，GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。详见 GitHub 隐私声明。你也可以直接在浮念的「设置」中阅读随应用提供的网页。查看浮念支持。

English

DriftNote is published by yang wang. This policy is effective November 24, 2026.

Entries and local settings

DriftNote 1.0 is a private journal for iPhone. It requires no account. The app uses the text, recordings, dates, categories, moods, and feelings you save to display, play back, and organize your entries. By default, it stores your entries and app preferences on your device. The app does not send your journal to the developer. It uses no ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics.

Microphone and device authentication

DriftNote uses the microphone when you choose to start a recording. It uses the audio for your entry and does not transcribe it. You can revoke microphone access in iPhone Settings. You can still access existing recordings, write, or save mood-only entries.

If you enable the privacy lock, iOS authenticates you. DriftNote receives the authentication result. It cannot read your facial or fingerprint data or your device passcode. After unlocking, you can turn the lock off in DriftNote Settings. The lock controls in-app access and does not add separate encryption to exported backups.

Apple services and manual backups

Entries start on this device. Check iCloud connection asks iOS whether iCloud Drive is available for your Apple Account. When you choose Enable iCloud sync, you can review existing entries, select local entries to add to sync, or receive entries already in your current Apple Account. After setup, newly saved entries can sync. DriftNote checks the account when syncing. Entry text, moods, dates, and recordings may be stored in your iCloud Drive. Entries you receive are stored on this iPhone. You can turn off sync in DriftNote Settings.

Apple device backups are separate from DriftNote sync. Depending on your Apple Account and device settings, device backups may include local DriftNote data. Apple handles data from those services under its privacy policy. The developer does not receive your system backup.

You can export a manual backup containing journal text, moods, and recordings. You choose where to save it in the system file picker, which can include a cloud storage location you select. The provider for that location handles the file under its own terms. When you select a backup to import, DriftNote reads and validates it before merging entries on the device. The import itself does not upload these entries. Backup operations create temporary copies. The app attempts to clear those copies and can prompt you to retry if cleanup fails.

Retention, deletion, and privacy questions

Saved entries stay on your device until you delete an entry or delete the app. Open an entry, tap the trash button, and confirm to delete the local entry and its recording. If audio cleanup does not finish, the app prompts you to retry. DriftNote keeps a local deletion marker so that importing an older backup can restore an entry as a recovered copy. Offload App in iPhone Settings can preserve app data. To remove the app's local data, choose Delete App instead. Deleting an entry or the app leaves separately exported files and copies in existing device backups in place. Manage those copies in their storage location or in the relevant device backup settings. If entries were published to iCloud, check the cloud copies separately. Local deletion or deleting the app does not guarantee immediate removal of every cloud copy.

If you delete an entry previously added to sync from this iPhone while sync is off, DriftNote keeps its category, creation time, and local deletion time for review when you turn sync back on with the same Apple Account. You can decide later. Only deletions you approve individually are queued for sync with that account.

Support email and public web pages

For help or privacy questions, email rac_wang@hotmail.com. The support mailbox stores your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send so the developer can reply and follow up. To request deletion of that correspondence, write from the address you used to contact support. Please keep journal entries, recordings, and backup files out of your message.

GitHub Pages hosts the public support page and privacy policy. When you visit in a browser, GitHub logs your IP address for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. You can also read the copies included with the app in DriftNote Settings. Visit DriftNote Support.
