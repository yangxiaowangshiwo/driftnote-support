浮念支持 / DriftNote Support

简体中文

联系开发者

如果需要帮助，可通过邮箱 rac_wang@hotmail.com 联系浮念开发者 yang wang。请描述遇到问题的页面和操作步骤，保留日记正文、录音及备份文件在你自己的设备上。

留下第一条记录

在主页点「留下这一刻」，选一种记录类别，再选择心情或继续到表达页。你可以写文字、录声音，也可以选好心情后留空正文。保存后，点当天的泡泡查看、编辑或删除。想按日期回顾时，打开「心路历程」。

录音和隐私锁

在表达页点「说出来」再点「开始录音」。如果之前拒绝了麦克风权限，可以在 iPhone「设置」的「隐私与安全性」中打开浮念的麦克风权限。隐私锁在浮念的齿轮「设置」中开启，使用 iPhone 提供的 Face ID、Touch ID 或设备密码。

换机前导出备份

在浮念「设置」点「导出备份」，按提示将 .driftnotebackup 文件保存到可在新 iPhone 上访问的位置。然后在新设备的浮念里点「导入备份」并选中该文件。浮念会验证备份，跳过重复记录，遇到内容冲突时保留恢复副本。导入的内容保存在本机，导入本身不会触发上传。备份包含私密内容，请妥善保管。

选择记录的保存位置

首次使用可以选「暂时仅保存在本机」。想使用当前 Apple 账户的 iCloud 云盘时，在浮念设置中点「开启 iCloud 同步」。如果本机和 iCloud 都没有记录，开启后保存的新记录可以同步。

如果已有记录，点「核对同步记录」。若一条本机记录与 iCloud 中的单个版本完全一致，可点「将这条记录与 iCloud 关联」。今后的修改可以同步。想同步部分本机记录时，勾选需要的记录，再点「将所选记录加入同步」。想接收 iCloud 中已有的记录时，保持本机记录未勾选，点「将 iCloud 记录同步到这台 iPhone」。如果看到「下载以核对」，先下载，再点「重新核对」。到达的记录可在主页查看。你也可以在设置中点「关闭 iCloud 同步」，继续使用本机记录。

本机和 iCloud 里的内容有差异

在「核对同步记录」里查看两份内容。如果两份内容不同，可以点「保留两个版本」。如果 iCloud 已有多个版本，逐项核对后点「保留所有版本」。回到主页可分别打开每份不同的内容。如果 iCloud 已删除一条记录，而这台 iPhone 上还有一份从未加入同步的正文，可点「保留本机记录并开启同步」。这份正文会继续只保存在这台 iPhone 上。

重新开启同步后，决定哪些删除要同步

如果你在关闭同步期间从本机删除了先前加入同步的记录，用原 Apple 账户重新开启同步后，可以逐条核对。清单显示类别、创建时间和本机删除时间。点「完成」可暂缓，稍后从「设置」进入「核对 iCloud 删除」。只有点「确认从 iCloud 删除这条记录」的项目才会排队同步删除；其他设备的处理时间取决于 iCloud。

English

Contact the developer

For help with DriftNote, email yang wang at rac_wang@hotmail.com. Tell me which screen you were on and what you tried. Please keep journal entries, recordings, and backup files on your device.

Save your first entry

Tap Save this moment on the home screen and pick a category. Choose a mood or continue to the next step. Write something, record a voice note, or leave the text blank after selecting a mood or feeling. After saving, tap that day's bubble to view, edit, or delete the entry. Open Your journey to browse earlier dates.

Recording and the privacy lock

On the final entry screen, select Speak, then Start recording. If you previously declined access, open Privacy & Security in iPhone Settings and allow microphone access for DriftNote. To enable the privacy lock, open DriftNote Settings using the gear. The lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, as available.

Make a backup before switching iPhones

In DriftNote Settings, tap Export backup and save the .driftnotebackup file somewhere you can access from your new iPhone. On the new device, tap Import backup in DriftNote and select the file. DriftNote validates the backup, skips duplicates, and keeps a recovered copy when entries conflict. Importing restores entries on this iPhone without uploading them. Keep the backup somewhere you trust.

Choose where to keep your entries

Choose Keep on this iPhone for now at first launch to keep entries locally. To use iCloud Drive with your current Apple Account, tap Enable iCloud sync in DriftNote Settings. If this iPhone and iCloud are both empty, new entries can sync after setup.

If you already have entries, tap Review records for iCloud sync. If an entry on this iPhone exactly matches a single version in iCloud, tap Link this record to iCloud to sync future edits. To sync some entries from this iPhone, select the ones you want and tap Add selected records to sync. To receive existing iCloud entries, leave the local entries unselected and tap Sync iCloud entries to this iPhone. If you see Download for review, download the entries and then tap Check again. Entries appear on the home screen as they arrive. To keep using your local entries with sync off, tap Turn off iCloud sync in Settings.

When this iPhone and iCloud have different versions

Open Review records for iCloud sync to compare the entries. If the two versions differ, choose Keep both versions. If iCloud already has several versions, review each one and choose Keep all versions. You can open each distinct version from the home screen. If an entry was deleted in iCloud and this iPhone still has a copy that was never added to sync, choose Keep local records and enable sync. That copy stays on this iPhone.

Choose which deletions to sync after turning sync back on

If you deleted entries previously added to sync from this iPhone while sync was off, turn sync back on with the same Apple Account to review them. Each entry shows its category, creation time, and the time you deleted it locally. Tap Done to decide later. You can reopen Review iCloud deletions in Settings. Only entries for which you tap Delete this entry from iCloud are queued for deletion. Other devices process the change when it reaches them through iCloud.
